    Who we help

    When Processing Work Competes With Account Servicing

    Policy downloads, certificate requests, supplemental applications and carrier follow-ups compete with insured enquiries and renewal conversations. The pressure shows up in everyday agency work.

    • Certificate interruptions

      Unplanned COI requests disrupt scheduled account servicing and renewal preparation.

    • Incomplete submissions

      Missing insured information triggers repeated follow-ups and delays quotation requests.

    • Carrier chasing

      Outstanding endorsements, policy documents and claim updates require repeated calls and emails.

    • AMS backlogs

      Unfiled documents and delayed updates make current policy information harder to retrieve.

    • Staff dependency

      When workflow knowledge sits with one CSR, leave or turnover can stall processing.

    • Hiring pressure

      Growing servicing volumes bring recruiting, training and supervision costs before additional capacity becomes productive.
